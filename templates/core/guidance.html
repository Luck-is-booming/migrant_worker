{% translate "Safer foreign employment, one step at a time" %}

  1. {% translate "Before deciding to work abroad" %}{% translate "Think about your goal, skills, health, family responsibilities, expected costs, and the risks you can accept." %}
  2. {% translate "Choose a legal and verified process" %}{% translate "Use official information and check whether the recruiter, institution, or program is authorized." %}
  3. {% translate "Check the recruiter and employer" %}{% translate "Confirm names, licenses where required, employer details, contact information, and official records." %}
  4. {% translate "Read the offer and contract carefully" %}{% translate "Compare the job, workplace, salary, deductions, working hours, leave, accommodation, insurance, and return terms." %}
  5. {% translate "Understand every cost" %}{% translate "Ask for a written list of charges and official receipts. Do not rely only on verbal promises or personal-account transfers." %}
  6. {% translate "Confirm labor approval and documents" %}{% translate "Check the required official approvals and keep safe copies of important documents before departure." %}
  7. {% translate "Understand insurance and available support" %}{% translate "Know what is covered, what is not covered, how to make a claim, and whom your family should contact." %}
  8. {% translate "Prepare before leaving" %}{% translate "Save emergency contacts, document copies, contract details, medicine information, and a safe way to stay in touch with family." %}
  9. {% translate "Protect yourself while working abroad" %}{% translate "Keep records of wages, working hours, messages, receipts, and any difference between your contract and actual work." %}
  10. {% translate "Get help when there is a problem or danger" %}{% translate "Put your immediate safety first and contact the relevant police, emergency service, embassy, labor office, medical service, or qualified professional." %}
  11. {% translate "Prepare to return home" %}{% translate "Plan your documents, final pay, claims, health needs, travel, and communication with your family." %}
  12. {% translate "After returning home" %}{% translate "Look for support with skills, work or business plans, finances, health, and family adjustment." %}
