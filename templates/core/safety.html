{% translate "Warning signs of fraud and unsafe job offers" %}

{% translate "One warning sign does not prove fraud, but it is a reason to stop, keep the evidence, and check through official sources before paying or agreeing." %}

{% translate "Pressure to pay immediately" %}

{% translate "Someone rushes you so you do not have time to check documents or ask for advice." %}

{% translate "No clear written documents" %}

{% translate "You are not given a clear job offer, receipt, contract, or employer details." %}

{% translate "Details do not match" %}

{% translate "The salary, job, workplace, fees, or benefits are different in messages, receipts, and contracts." %}

{% translate "Payment to a personal account" %}

{% translate "Money is requested through a personal account without a clear official reason and receipt." %}

{% translate "Requests for OTPs or passwords" %}

{% translate "A genuine counselor or office should not ask for your password, OTP, bank PIN, or social-media login." %}

{% translate "Original documents are kept" %}

{% translate "Someone keeps your original documents without a clear lawful reason or safe return process." %}

{% translate "The employer cannot be verified" %}

{% translate "You cannot confirm who the employer is, where the workplace is, or who signed the offer." %}

{% translate "The offer exists only on social media" %}

{% translate "The job comes from an unverified account and cannot be confirmed anywhere trustworthy." %}

{% translate "Promises that sound too good to be true" %}

{% translate "Someone promises certain approval, unusually high income, or an easy process with no normal checks." %}

{% translate "What to do" %}

{% translate "If you think something may be wrong" %}

  1. {% translate "Stop paying" %}{% translate "Do not send more money until you have checked the situation, when it is safe to stop." %}
  2. {% translate "Keep the evidence" %}{% translate "Save receipts, messages, names, phone numbers, documents, account details, and dates." %}
  3. {% translate "Protect private information" %}{% translate "Do not post private identity, banking, or contact details publicly." %}
  4. {% translate "Ask an official office for help" %}{% translate "Contact the relevant government office, police, embassy, labor authority, or qualified professional." %}
